Transfers Out Information

If you decide you want to transfer your pension we’ll send you a transfer pack which you and your new provider will need to complete and return to us so we can complete the transfer.

Alternatively, your receiving scheme may be able to request the transfer of funds via a system called Origo, which is also known as Options. This means you won’t need to fill in any forms for us, but not all schemes are able to do this so you’ll need to check with your receiving scheme.
